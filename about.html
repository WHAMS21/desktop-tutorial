The idea behind WHAMSY

Home should feel a little like a daydream.

WHAMSY starts with a simple thought: the things we rest on can bring comfort and personality into the same room. Pillows become more than practical; they can be gentle, expressive and unmistakably yours.

The first collection is imagined around four moods: the calm of REST, the details of HOME, the playfulness of PLAY and the wonder of DREAMS. The brand is being built from Kenya, with original characters and a welcoming spirit at its heart.
