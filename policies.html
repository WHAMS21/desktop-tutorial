A clear note for our visitors

Shop information.

Here is how the WHAMSY catalogue and WhatsApp order request work.

Home   /   Shop information

Products and prices

While the site is in local preview mode, the included product names, images and KSh prices are examples only. Once the online catalogue is connected, WHAMSY manages what is published and whether prices are shown. A published price still needs availability and delivery confirmation in WhatsApp. Adding an item to your bag does not reserve stock.

Orders and payment

Checkout opens a draft order request to WHAMSY on WhatsApp. It is not a completed order or a payment. WHAMSY confirms stock, final amount and delivery, then shares the payment instructions. This website does not collect or confirm M-Pesa payments.

Delivery and returns

Delivery areas, charges, timelines and return terms have not been confirmed. WHAMSY must publish these details before accepting orders.

Privacy in this preview

The bag stores product IDs and quantities in this browser. Name, phone and delivery details are placed in a WhatsApp message only after you press the order button and choose to send it. They are not stored by the website. Do not include card details or M-Pesa PINs in WhatsApp.

Before the shop opens

WHAMSY will need to add verified support channels, real product photography and specifications, supplier and stock information, delivery rules, approved policies, business details, and secure payment and order handling.

This page describes the current site preview. It is not a live sales offer or a substitute for final customer terms.
